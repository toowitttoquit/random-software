## Origin Story

People building with AI are making real things, often without a developer's background. GitHub wasn't built for them, and social feeds reward whatever spreads fastest rather than whatever is most interesting.

Is This Anything? is a place to ask the question in its name: to show something half-finished and find out whether it's worth finishing.

## What It Does

### Share Your Progress

Give a work-in-progress a proper home, with a presentation that does it justice.

### Get Feedback

Hear from other builders about what's working, without an algorithm deciding who sees it.

### Find Collaborators

Discover people building things you care about, and work on them together.
